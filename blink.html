You typed it. Someone told you to, or you worked it out. Either way you are paying the kind of attention this was built to find.

Here is what nobody warns you about being watched: you stop doing the ordinary things on your own. You hold your breath without deciding to. You forget to blink until it stings. The body waits for permission it is not going to get.

The ones in the doorway never do it at all. That is how you tell them from a reflection. A reflection blinks when you blink. They hold the eye open and they do not look away and they do not blink.

If your eyes have started to sting for no reason, or you have caught yourself counting, that is worth writing down. Then say it back.
