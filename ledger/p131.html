One stroke. One ball. Ball at the foot — top block, the first sixteen. Ball at the head — the second block. A word opens with the little ring, the o.

Position in the block is the short marks across the stroke. Nothing — first. A mark high on the stroke — one along. A mark low — two. Both marks — three. One axis walks across, the other walks down, and the two axes trade sides when the ball moves to the head. So a bare stroke with a ball at the foot is the very first letter and nothing else.
