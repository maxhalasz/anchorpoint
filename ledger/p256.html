p. 256

the network

late. this is the page I least wanted to write and the one I most needed to.

PHOTO: Maxa full page, tight late hand, a small diagram: a front, a filter, a scatter of dots labelled “readers”

I could not keep the archive. One person, one flat, one lock is a single point of failure, and I have read enough of these files to know what happens to single points of failure. So I did not keep it. I moved it. Not to a place — to people. People I will never meet, chosen only by whether they can hold what I hand them without dropping it and without being crushed under it.

The shape: a front that filters. A quiet way through for the ones the front lets past. Fragments that only assemble in the right head. You have been inside this the whole time. The place you came from is one wall of it and this page is another.

I think the cipher keeps the secret. I think it carries it far enough to arrive whole. I have to think that.
