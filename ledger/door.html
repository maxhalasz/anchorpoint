V · The Door

municipal records, sub-level. i worked there fourteen months before i saw it

A maintenance door, painted the same green as the wall, no handle plate. I had walked past it every day. The morning I finally saw it properly, there was a mark on it at eye height — the word — and I understood that no one else in the building had ever seen the door at all.

It was held shut by an ordinary lock and by something that is not ordinary: a concealment, a working, the kind of thing the DRE filed under one word. I only get through it because of what happened to me when I was eight.

The word for it is ████████. Behind it: paper, floor to ceiling.

it is written on the door itself. and one line in the Names decodes to it.

The lock gives. The other thing does not resist me. It never has.

What is behind the door is the rest of this — how I moved it out, who is holding it now, and why it has to keep moving.
