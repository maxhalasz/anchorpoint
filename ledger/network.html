VI · The Network

how it leaves my hands without leaving a trail

I could not keep the archive. One person, one flat, one lock — that is a single point of failure and I have read enough of these files to know what happens to single points of failure.

So I did not keep it. I moved it. Not to a place — to people. People I do not know and will never meet, chosen by whether they can carry the weight of what I hand them without dropping it or being crushed. The cipher is the container. If it reaches the other side intact, the reader was the right reader.

the shape of it

A front that filters. A quiet channel for the ones who pass the filter. Fragments that only assemble in the right head. You have been inside this the whole time. The page you came from is one wall of it.

I think the cipher keeps the secret. I think it carries it far enough to arrive whole. I have to think that.
