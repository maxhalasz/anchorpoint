trauma, induced

the oldest one I bothered to keep. torn where I tore it.

PHOTO: Maxtop half of a page, biro, very early — tidy, almost relieved

Everything from the shelter, the boxes, the men with the raised arms, the thing going into the mouth — induced. A false memory laid down over a real fright. He explained the mechanism and it held together and I let it hold for about ten years.

I feel better having written this down. I did. That was the point of it. See p.288.
