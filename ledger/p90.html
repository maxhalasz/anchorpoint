the reporter

FREELANCE WRITER, 44, FOUND DEAD IN MOTEL BATH — RULED SUICIDE
… had spent three years on a single sprawling story he referred to, to friends, as "the octopus." Associates said he had travelled to West Virginia to meet a source. He left no note.

Days earlier he had told more than one friend the same thing: that if he were ever reported to have taken his own life, they were not to believe it.

He had a week or two more of the story than I think I have of mine. I am not being dramatic. I am doing the arithmetic.
