VII · The Doctor

i am adding this page against my own advice

His name is Dr. Ryan Pephal. He took me on when the last one retired. He is the one who named the Unusual Friend, who said the dreams sharpening was the new medication doing its work, who wrote the prescription for Aklo by name.

I have never waited more than a day for an appointment. The office is always where it was. He is always expecting me.

I wrote his name backwards once, in the margin, to see it fresh. I do not like what it nearly spells. I have not gone back.

the arrangement, as I understand it now

The dreams are not clearing because of a pill. The pill is so that I will say it is the pill. The day I stop believing that — the day I sit in that chair and doubt him to his face — there will be no office, no file, no Dr. Pephal. I know this the way I know about the door.

I have not doubted him yet. Draw your own line from that.
