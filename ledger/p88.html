It goes out as a grant, comes back as a consultancy fee, leaves again as equipment nobody unpacks. Every box I draw turns out to have two more boxes inside it. Meridian Hand collapsed the year a founder went into a lake. Halcyon‑Vantage is listed as the largest holder of all three shells and of six thousand things that are not shells. Caselight is the case software the DRE used to run the archive, and I am fairly sure the archive was never the point of the software.
