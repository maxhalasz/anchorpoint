p. 212

the corridor

PHOTO: Maxa page half in English half in the other script — the foreign lines dense, drawn not written, some retraced

The corridor again. Doors on one side only. I never reach the end. Lately I wake with my hand already moving and this is on the page:

It is not code. I have tried it as code for a year. It is a language and I do not have it — I have maybe nine words of it, the way you have nine words of a place you changed planes in once. This clump I think is a name. This one I think is a place. I could be wrong about all of it.

Someone was talking in my sleep, in my voice. My wife heard it before I did. The doctor calls it an Unusual Friend — a part of me helping me make sense of things.
