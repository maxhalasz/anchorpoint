the doctor

PHOTO: Maxa full page, unsteady late hand, one word written large and then written again backwards beside it

Dr Ryan Pephal. Took me on when the last one retired. He named the Unusual Friend. He said the dreams sharpening was the medication working. He wrote AKLO by name.

I have never waited more than a day for an appointment. The office is always where it was. He is always expecting me.

I wrote his name backwards once, in the margin, to see it fresh. I do not like what it nearly spells. I have not gone back to look again.

Here is the arrangement as I understand it now. The dreams are not clearing because of a pill. The pill is so that I will say it is the pill. The day I stop saying that — the day I sit in that chair and doubt him to his face — there will be no office, no file, no Dr Pephal. I know this the way I know about the door.

I have not doubted him yet. Draw your own line from that.
