—

If you are reading my handwriting then the front let you through, which means you are the kind of person it was built to let through. I am not going to explain that.

These are pages out of something much larger. I send the ones I can. I do not send them whole and I do not send them in sequence, because sequence is the first thing they go looking for. Number them if it helps you. It does not help me anymore.

The psychiatrist is almost certainly right about all of this. I was thirty-four when I wrote that line. Different pen now. He was not right.

There is a mark. I keep it on another page. I did not understand yet that the mark and the door were the same fact.
