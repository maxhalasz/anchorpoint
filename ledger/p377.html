This is the part I could get out. The rest is where it has to stay — in pieces, in people, moving. If you have read this far you are one of the people now, whether you meant to be or not.

I am not going to tell you how much there is. Counting it was the last thing that still frightened me and I would like to keep it that way.

Keep it moving. Do not keep it. — and if you are ever told I stopped on my own, you already know what to do with that.
