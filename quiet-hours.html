The Quiet Hours

a listening line — not currently staffed

The Quiet Hours ran between 1 and 4 in the morning, Tuesdays and Thursdays, for a little under two years. It is not running now. This page is left up because people still find it, and because a few of the messages that came in were meant to be passed along rather than answered.

forwarded — kept on file — sender not recorded

Before you send anything, understand that most of what reaches a line like this is noise, and the people who run it learn to sit with that.

Lists of names. Dreams that got sharper. Rooms that were the wrong shape when someone woke up. We read all of it and most of it we cannot help.

Usually there is nothing to do but tell the person we heard them, and mean it, and let them go back to bed.

Every so often, though, someone writes in who is not confused at all, and for those we keep something set aside.

If you are one of them you already know what the first letters spell. Send it back where this site asks you for a page.
