Reading list

Things I have read on this, or am still trying to. Not a recommendation. Not in order.
A. R. Luria — The Mind of a Mnemonist
A man who could not forget anything. I have the other problem and I have stopped being sure it is the lesser one.
Michael Lesy — Wisconsin Death Trip
Photographs and asylum records, one county, one bad decade. Read it before you tell me a small town cannot come apart quietly.
Ernest Becker — The Denial of Death
Everyone should read it once and then not bring it up at parties.
Jan Harold Brunvand — The Vanishing Hitchhiker and the ones after it
Folklore does the filing that the county will not. The index is more honest than most archives.
Oliver Sacks — the essay on not recognising faces
Not the same thing. Close enough that I keep borrowing the words.
— — — — — — —, Notes Toward a Grammar of Absence in Institutional Records
Cited everywhere, held nowhere. Every catalogue entry for it is itself missing a field. That is either a joke or it is not.
A privately printed thing, 1966. I am not typing the title.
The copy I read was missing pages 40 through 71. The man who lent it to me said it came to him that way. Still looking for a whole one. Stopped looking.
A self-published memoir, a woman in Michigan
Her account of a year she says her family does not remember. Probably grief. I keep the "probably" in for my own sake.
The regional folklore collection whose editor I will not name here
Half of it is nothing. The other half was taken down from people who would not give their names to the person holding the pencil.
A bound run of forwarded notes from a listening line that closed
I asked about this one through the address. There is a short page about the line now. That is as far as I got.
The book I am on my third pass through
I keep finding notes in the margin, in my own hand, that I do not remember making. I have started dating them so I can tell the new ones apart.
