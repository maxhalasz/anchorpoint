I have never been to that town. I do not need to have been. I have a file on a different place that fits the same shape, and the shape is the point.

People near a certain kind of site stop being looked at. First the eye slides off them in a room. Then the people who love them look straight at them and see a coat over a chair, a shadow, the wall — whatever is dull enough to fill the gap. By the time anyone would think to file a report there is no one in the house who remembers there was someone to file about.

The last thing to go wrong, in the two accounts I have that got that far, is the mouth. It keeps coming in. New teeth behind the old ones, rows of them, and the person stops being a face in the doorway and becomes just the mouth in the doorway, and then they are gone and unremembered and the water never gives anything back.

I tried to hold one of the names from that file in my head for an hour. It went soft in my hands like a wet page. If you have had an hour like that — a name, a face, a room that was the wrong size when you woke — you are not losing your mind. You have been standing too close to something. Write it down. Then say it back.
