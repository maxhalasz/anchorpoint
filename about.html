this page is edited rarely, and on purpose
This is the most of my face I am comfortable putting somewhere it can be copied.

I do not use my name here. If you have one for me from somewhere else, keep it to yourself. It does me no good to find it typed into a box on my own site, and it does you no good either. A name is a way in. This whole place is built to have as few of those as I can manage.

AnchorPoint runs off a home computer that is older than it has any right to be, on a connection I understand end to end. That is not nostalgia. A newer machine is a machine other people can reach. If the page looks like it was put together twenty years ago, good — that was the last cosmetic decision I plan to make about it.

There was a bad year. I am not going to lay it out here. What matters is how it ended: with me holding things I was not meant to have, and then a long stretch of trying to read further and finding every next door shut, sealed, or already gone. I know a little. I have made my peace with the size of the little.

What I learned that was worth keeping, I keep. Not on this site. This site is the front of the house. If you have written in and had nothing back, that is not a fault on your end or mine — the line only goes one way, and it is meant to. I read everything that arrives. I cannot answer, and if I could I would not, because an answer is a thread and threads get pulled.

If you want to see what you have sent from this machine, there is a page for it. It is called by the four words you would say handing someone a sheet of paper: you sent this one. Type that where the site asks you for a page.

— The Author

added later

I wrote above that I read all of it. Still true. It has got harder to do that and stay level. If your message was one of the bad ones and you are down here reading the small print: I did read it. Twice.
