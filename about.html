This is the most of my face I am comfortable putting somewhere it can be copied.

I do not use my name here. If you have one for me from somewhere else, keep it to yourself. It does me no good to find it typed into a box on my own site, and it does you no good either. A name is a way in. This whole place was built as a safe haven, a place where people can find comfort with their own.

AnchorPoint runs off a home computer that is older than anything else I own, on a connection I understand end to end. If the page looks like it was put together a few years ago, please do not complain, that was the last cosmetic decision I plan to make about it. Give my old box a break.

I had a bad couple of years. I am not going to lay it out here and make this about me. What matters is how it ended: with me finally taking a hold of things I thought I would never have, and the a long stretch of trying to read further and finding every next door shut, sealed, or already gone.

I read everything that arrives. I usually cannot answer, and if I could I would not, because an answer is a thread and threads get pulled, but we, as a community, and a team make sure to be there for you.

— The Author
