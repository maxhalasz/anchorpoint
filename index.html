Welcome to AnchorPoint

If you have made it here, chances are you are carrying a weight you feel you cannot share with anyone else. I know exactly what that feels like. I know how the silence of the night can be deafening, and how our own mind can sometimes turn into the most hostile place in the world.

For a long time, I kept everything to myself. I thought that admitting what was going on inside my head was a declaration of defeat, a proof of weakness. But the truth is that no one was made to carry the weight of the world alone. This space was born from a moment of personal ruin and, right after, from a series of discoveries that changed my life.

You do not need to have all the answers today. You do not need to fix everything now. Sometimes the first step to finding peace is not fighting the inner noise, but learning to sit down and talk with your own head. Make yourself at home. This place is yours.

— The Author

If it helps to know
  • In a given year, roughly one in five adults lives with a mental health condition.
  • Close to 60% of them will never talk to a professional about it.
  • Depression is among the leading causes of disability worldwide.
  • For people aged 15 to 29, suicide is one of the top causes of death.
  • The typical delay between first symptoms and first treatment is about a decade.
  • Most people improve with support — plain support, not necessarily treatment.
  • Reaching out once makes reaching out again easier. Blink for one second and it might just be gone.
